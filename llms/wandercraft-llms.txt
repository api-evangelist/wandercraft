# Wandercraft

> Wandercraft is a robotics company founded in 2012 by Nicolas Simon, Matthieu Masselin and
> Jean-Louis Constanza, dual-headquartered in Paris and New York with 175+ AI, robotics and
> industrial staff. It builds self-balancing robotic exoskeletons and humanoid robots:
> Atalante X (hands-free overground rehabilitation exoskeleton, CE-marked 2019, FDA-cleared
> for stroke 2022, spinal cord injury 2024 and multiple sclerosis 2025, in 100+ rehabilitation
> and research centers), Eve (personal self-balancing exoskeleton for wheelchair users,
> FDA-cleared, US commercial launch 2026), and Calvin-40 (industrial humanoid for part
> handling, flow logistics, picking and kitting, deployed at Renault's Douai plant).
